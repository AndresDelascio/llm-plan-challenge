VERDICT: PROCEED WITH CHANGES

### 1. Pass line does not say which run it judges
- Severity: major
- Claim: Step 5 produces two results (engine off, engine on) and step 1 names neither, nor the denominator for "outcomes right" (all shots or found kicks).
- Why it matters: If one run passes and the other fails, or 50/60 (83%) versus 50/57 (88%) straddle the line, the choice is made after seeing results and there is no single answer.
- Fix: In step 1, state "engine on, all labelled shots as denominator" and write thresholds as counts (e.g. ≥57 found, ≥51 right, ≤1 false kick).

### 2. No rule for re-clicks, reruns or dropped shots
- Severity: major
- Claim: Nothing fixes how many ball-click attempts or analysis runs are allowed per video, or which shots may be excluded.
- Why it matters: A bad first click invites a retry and awkward shots invite exclusion; both are tuning on report footage by the back door.
- Fix: Write before recording: one click, one run per configuration, every recorded shot counts, exclusions only at labelling time before any automatic run.

### 3. Outcome mix is uncontrolled
- Severity: major
- Claim: "A mix" sets no minimum for misses and woodwork.
- Why it matters: If 50 of 60 shots are goals, 85% is reachable while most misses and woodwork are wrong, and the comparison with the benchmark is not like for like.
- Fix: Set minimum counts per outcome in step 1, close to the benchmark's proportions, and report accuracy per outcome.

### 4. 60 shots cannot separate pass from fail near the line
- Severity: major
- Claim: The binary verdict ignores the sampling error the plan already acknowledges.
- Why it matters: An analyzer truly at 80% still scores ≥51/60 about one time in five, so a reported "pass" may be overturned by a repeat.
- Fix: Predefine an inconclusive band (e.g. 48–53 right) that triggers more sessions, and report the 95% interval beside the percentage.

### 5. Location is not pinned
- Severity: minor
- Claim: The plan does not say the new position is at the same goal and facility as the benchmark.
- Why it matters: A fail at another venue cannot be attributed to camera position, which is the question asked.
- Fix: Record at the benchmark goal with the same ball, changing only the tripod position.
