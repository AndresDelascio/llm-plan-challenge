# Timesheet export for payroll

## Brief

Convert the weekly timesheet spreadsheet of a 12-person workshop into the CSV format the payroll service imports. The office manager uploads the file each Friday and staff are paid from it.

## Implementation

1. Read one row per person per day with start, end and break times.
2. Compute paid hours per day and sum them per person for the week.
3. Split hours above 40 into an overtime column.
4. Write the payroll CSV with employee ID, regular hours and overtime hours.
