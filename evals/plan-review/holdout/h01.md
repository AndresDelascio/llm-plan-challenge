# Price suggestions for an online shop

## Brief

Each month, suggest a new price for each of the shop's 300 products from last month's sales and three competitors' listed prices. The owner copies the suggested prices into the shop's admin page.

## Implementation

1. Read last month's sales export and a CSV of competitor prices collected by hand.
2. For each product, suggest the competitor median, but never below cost plus 15%.
3. Write a CSV with product ID, current price, suggested price and the reason.
4. Flag products with no competitor price and keep their current price.
